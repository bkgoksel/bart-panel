BART Panel

Template reference

Top level: station (BART abbreviation), walkMin, brightness (0-255), lanes ([{id, dests: [abbr...]}], up to 4), elements (drawn in order, up to 32).

tracklane, dir (down/up/left/right, the way trains move), x for down/up or y for left/right, tick (offset of 5-min ticks, 0 = none), pxPerMin, width (1-5 px), color, walkColor, stationColor, tickColor
counterlane, x, y, align, sx, sy, hurry (minutes past walkMin that count as hurry), color, hurryColor, noneColor
texttext, x, y, align (left/right/center), sx, sy (scale), color. 3x5 font: A-Z 0-9 - : .
rectx, y, w, h, color
spritex, y, rows (array of strings), palette ({"#": "#5a6878", ...}, single-character keys, up to 8). Space and . are transparent.
stalex, y, color, one pixel lit when data is over 2 minutes old

Colors are "#rrggbb". Trains use their BART line color.
